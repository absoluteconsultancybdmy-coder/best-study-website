## Safety and Usage

- Teacher visuals are fictional editorial illustrations, not representations of the real teacher.
- The homepage labels teacher visuals and course covers as illustrative.
- No teacher identity, credential, institution, result, statistic, price, phone number, email address, or testimonial is embedded in an image.
- No remote image URL or Google-hosted asset is used.
- The same teacher portrait supplies the compact avatar crop and full teacher-area visual until an approved real teacher photo is provided.

## Prompt Set

1. Generic Bangladeshi teacher beside a classroom chalkboard, clearly illustrative and non-identifying, using the Best Study green, saffron, cream, and warm-neutral palette.
